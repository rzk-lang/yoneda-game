title: Morphisms, triangles, and functions
---

Here type theory turns **directed**. A type now carries not just points and paths but oriented cells: **morphisms** and the **triangles** between them. This section builds those cells by hand, then shows that a function carries them along. First you construct identity morphisms and fill degenerate triangles, needing no hypothesis on the type. Then a function `g : A → B` acts on whole cells, not just points: this is **functoriality**. Everything here is free. The Segal hypothesis that makes arrows composable waits for the next section.
