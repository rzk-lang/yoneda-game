title: Wrap-up
---

In a Segal type, composition exists and comes with a witnessing triangle, the two halves of one center of contraction. That is what makes a Segal type a **pre-$(\infty,1)$-category**; a **Rezk type**, a Segal type that is also locally univalent, is then a genuine $(\infty,1)$-category.

Uniqueness of composites does the real work of the unit laws. It says the chosen composite is the *only* witnessed composite, so any triangle you can build pins its hypotenuse to it. The two unit laws are immediate corollaries: the degenerate unit triangles witness `f` as a composite with the identity, and uniqueness identifies that composite with `f`. They also feed the directed development ahead. The left unit law closes the easy round-trip of the Yoneda equivalence, and the right unit law simplifies the naturality square for representable transformations. The next chapter proves the Yoneda lemma.
