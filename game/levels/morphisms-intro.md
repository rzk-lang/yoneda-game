title: Morphisms and triangles
---

In directed type theory a **morphism** $x \to y$ is a path along the directed interval $\Delta^1$, and a **triangle** (`hom2`) is a map out of the $2$-simplex $\Delta^2$. We build them by hand: the identity morphism, the constant triangle, and a degenerate unit triangle filled by reparametrizing an edge. The mirror **left-unit** triangle is marked ★, optional enrichment you may skip.
