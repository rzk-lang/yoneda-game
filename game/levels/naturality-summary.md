title: Wrap-up
---

You proved that every fiberwise transformation between representable functors is automatically natural. No naturality hypothesis was needed: the Segal structure supplies it. The argument was a single square, built from a composition witness and a unit triangle and transported through `ϕ`. One lemma about squares, proved once by uniqueness of composites, turned it into an equality, and the right unit law and path concatenation finished the job.

This is the technical heart of the contravariant Yoneda lemma. With naturality automatic, the Yoneda maps can be defined without carrying a separate naturality proof, and the equivalence between a representable transformation and its value at the identity follows next.
