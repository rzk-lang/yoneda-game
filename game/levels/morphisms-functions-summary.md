---

You can now build morphisms as paths and fill triangles by reusing an edge under a change of coordinates, and you have seen a function carry those cells along, sending points to points and morphisms to morphisms. All of it was free, needing no hypothesis on the type. The next section adds the one hypothesis that makes arrows composable: the Segal condition.
