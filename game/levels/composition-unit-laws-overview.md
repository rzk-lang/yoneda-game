title: Composition and unit laws
---

So far every cell was free. Genuine **composition** needs a hypothesis: a type is **Segal** when each composable pair of arrows has a unique filler triangle, and the composite is read off the center of that contractible space. This section extracts the composite and the triangle that witnesses it, then uses the *uniqueness* of that composite to prove the **unit laws**. Composing with an identity changes nothing, and uniqueness is what turns that intuition into an equality.
